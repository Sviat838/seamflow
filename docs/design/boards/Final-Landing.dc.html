§ 3 — Certificates

Certify it. Print it. Put it on the product.

Every verdict comes with a Certificate of Classification and its own QR code. Print the QR on your packaging, menus and food-truck windows, and your customers can scan it to confirm a certified hot dog.

  1. 1

    Classify the product

    Submit a product photo. HDX returns a verdict under Policy v3.2.

  2. 2

    A certificate is issued

    A signed one-page PDF with a seal, a QR and a verification code.

  3. 3

    Print QR labels

    Label sheets at 25, 40 or 60 mm, or vector files for your designer.

  4. 4

    Customers scan to verify

    A phone camera opens hdx.example/v/7KQ2-M9XD, the public record.

Certificates can be revoked. If the Hot Dog Review Board overturns a verdict on appeal, its certificate is revoked and every label shows Revoked on the next scan.
