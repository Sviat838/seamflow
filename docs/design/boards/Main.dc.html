Product decisions

  1. 1

    Upload first. No signup, with 20 free classifications.

  2. 2

    Binary verdict, backed by policy. Hot Dog Definition Policy v3.2 settles edge cases, and the Hot Dog Review Board hears appeals.

  3. 3

    Live global feed. Anonymised: region, verdict and confidence, never images.

  4. 4

    QR certificates. A PDF certificate, plus QR labels businesses print on their products. Shoppers scan to verify, and certificates can be revoked.

  5. 5

    Business plan. API, batch, certificates, SSO and SLA. Pricing per customer: contact us.
